A caveat: priorities don't always align

In software engineering, the work that helps users or teams most doesn't always make the most money. Work that is intrinsically meaningful to an engineer or extrinsically valuable to users may be deprioritized when it has no clear path to revenue, while revenue-generating work can take priority even when it isn't the most useful.
