TL;DR: what might make work exciting for you?

You seem most excited by work that gives you an interesting challenge and makes something better for people. Helping lots of people matters, but may not be enough if the work itself doesn’t engage you. This is a personal pattern, not a rule or a measure of how important a task is.

How the score works

Intrinsic averages subject interest and technical challenge (both rated from 0 to 10). Extrinsic is impact per user × reach ÷ 10, where impact is −10 to +10 and reach is 0 to 10. Overall is 55% intrinsic + 45% extrinsic, giving intrinsic a slight preference.

For each component, effort adjustment is score − (hours ÷ 8) × (1 − 0.5 × score ÷ 10). At a component score of 0, each 8 hours costs 1 point; higher scores reduce that cost and lower scores increase it.

Deadline factor is 0.25 + 0.75 × min(available ÷ estimated, 1). It scales a positive effort-adjusted overall score, reaching 0.25× when no time is available. Negative scores are left unchanged so deadline pressure cannot make a net-negative result appear less negative.

Available time affects the deadline factor; it does not change the effort-adjusted component scores.
